How this was scored

Evidence-based, not self-reported. 143 topics across 12 domains curated from NVIDIA DLI coursework, AWS AI Practitioner certification, OWASP/NIST/ISO frameworks, and current AI/ML research literature. A topic only reaches dark green with a shipped artifact, a debugging session that went past the surface, or a session where you taught it to someone else. Light green means you've reasoned about it correctly. Light red means surface-level exposure only. Red means no demonstrated knowledge.

The known bias

This maps what you demonstrated during the assessment. Work you did silently — at TRP, in personal projects, or in research you didn't reference — is invisible here and will be scored too low. Treat red cells in your own domain as prompts to provide evidence, not as verdicts.

Reading breadth vs depth

Breadth is how much of the board is not red. Depth is how much is dark green. A wide band of light green with few dark cells is the signature of someone who reads and explains faster than they build — the fix is fewer new topics, more finished ones.
